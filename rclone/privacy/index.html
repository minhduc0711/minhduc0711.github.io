Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how this website handles information. The site is the public home page for a personal Google OAuth client used with rclone. It is not the official rclone website and is not affiliated with the rclone project.

Effective date: October 18, 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by minhduc0711. It provides the public home page and privacy policy for a personal Google OAuth client that its owner uses with rclone, a separate open-source command-line program. This website is not the official rclone project and is not affiliated with it.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to this website at https://minhduc0711.github.io/rclone/ and to support messages you send us. It does not apply to rclone, Google Cloud, Google Drive, GitHub, or other third-party services, which have their own policies.

3. Information we collect

This is a static website. It does not provide a login, forms, analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. We do not receive your Google password, OAuth client ID or secret, OAuth tokens, or Google Drive files through this website.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamps to deliver and secure the site, under GitHub's privacy policy.

If you email us or open a public issue, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, GitHub username, message, and attachments. Do not send passwords, client secrets, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

4. Google user data and OAuth

This website does not integrate with Google OAuth and does not receive Google user data. You create the OAuth client ID and secret in your own Google Cloud project and store them in your local rclone configuration.

When you authorize rclone, the flow takes place directly between your device and Google. Any Google Drive data that rclone reads or modifies is accessed by rclone on your device, not through this website.

To the extent Google user data is processed through the OAuth client, it is used only to provide the rclone functionality you request. It is not sold or used for advertising, and it is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. It is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide the requested functionality, comply with law, or with your consent. This is consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. How we use information

We use the limited information described above only to operate this website, respond to support or privacy requests, protect against abuse, and comply with legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

6. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with the hosting and communication providers needed to run this site, or when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request, or when necessary to protect rights, safety, or security. We do not share Google user data because this website does not receive it.

7. Data retention and security

This website does not maintain a database of user information. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, and meet legal obligations. Hosting logs are retained under GitHub's practices.

The site is served over HTTPS and contains only static content. It does not accept or store credentials. You are responsible for protecting your Google account, OAuth client secret, tokens, and local rclone configuration.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information we hold, or to request a portable copy. Contact us to exercise these rights. Because this site does not collect account or Google user data, we may not hold information about you beyond messages you have sent us.

You can revoke the OAuth client's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can delete the corresponding credentials and tokens from your local rclone configuration.

To request deletion of support messages we hold, email phamminhduc0711@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.

9. Cookies and children's privacy

This website does not use cookies for advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Third-party links and changes

This website links to Google Cloud, Google Account settings, GitHub, and rclone.org. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We may update this policy from time to time and will change the effective date above when we do.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email phamminhduc0711@gmail.com or open an issue at github.com/minhduc0711/minhduc0711.github.io/issues. Do not include passwords, client secrets, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials in a public issue.
